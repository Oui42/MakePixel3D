1. WHAT THE PROGRAM DOES

MakePixel3D turns a photo into a 3D model using artificial intelligence and renders it as pixel art. All processing takes place on your computer. The program does not send your photos or your work anywhere. An internet connection is used only to install components and to check for updates.

3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

The 3D model and the pixel art are generated automatically. The back and hidden parts of an object are guessed by the AI and may be inaccurate. You are responsible for checking the results before using them and for making sure you have the right to use the photos you process.

5. INSTALLATION AND DATA

The installer downloads about 4 GB from python.org, pypi.org, download.pytorch.org, github.com and huggingface.co. Your gallery, settings and logs are stored on your computer: next to the program when that folder is writable, otherwise in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MakePixel3D. Uninstalling the program keeps your gallery and settings.


6. UPDATES

The program checks for new versions on GitHub at startup (this can be turned off in the settings) and installs them only when you confirm.
